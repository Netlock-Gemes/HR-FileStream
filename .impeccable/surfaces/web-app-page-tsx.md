Scope: HR Filestream viewer site (home, browse, title, cast, list, search), player, login, admin dashboard. Mode: Operate for browse and dashboard, Experience for home, title and player.
Audience: mostly the owner, evenings, phone and desktop equally. Job: find something and get it playing in two taps; curate the library.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A precise catalogue. The library reads like a well-set index: hairline grid, tight bold type, measurements in mono, one signal orange for the thing you act on. Refuses the dark streaming-store grid of rounded cards and gradient heroes.
OWN-WORLD: Off-white #fafaf8 ground, white paper cells, ink #0c0c0c, signal orange #ff5a00 only for Play, progress, current page and pressed filters. Inter Tight 800 headlines at -0.045em, JetBrains Mono for runtimes, quality, counts and dates. Thin 1px rules bound every cell; 3-4px radii; no shadows except menus.
STORY: Arrive on the newest title already set as an index entry; Play is one tap; sections below are labelled rows; Browse is the full index with real pages, sorts, shuffle and filters; the dashboard is the same index with editing.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: split hero, copy panel left (numbered position, 4.8rem headline, mono facts, Play + Details, numbered list of the other new titles with a reel bar), still right. Phone: still on top, copy below, prev/next arrows, bottom tab bar.
FORM: Swiss index, chosen by the user from five light looks after rejecting the dark Projection Room. seed: b26b3f1dadc5eeb7
SIGNATURE: The numbered index list in the hero with an orange reel line that fills as the hero advances; grids drawn as hairline cells like a catalogue page.
